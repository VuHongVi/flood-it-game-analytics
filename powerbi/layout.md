## Page: Growth

### Daily active players and installs

- Visual: Line chart (DAU) above a clustered column chart (Installs), same x-axis; spike days as orange markers
- Fields: Axis: fact_daily_activity[date]; Values: [DAU] and, separately, [Installs]; conditional marker colour by is_spike_day
- Definition: DAU = distinct players with >= 1 event on the event_date. Installs = players whose first_open falls on the day.
- Source: `fact_daily_activity.csv (dau, installs, is_spike_day)`
- Insight (verbatim): DAU averages 471.4 on normal days. The 7 spike days (~1,229 DAU, ~1.6 installs) are an artefact: screen_view falls to 14.5% of normal and the fixed 50,000-event sample spreads over more players.

### Stickiness (DAU / MAU)

- Visual: Line chart
- Fields: Axis: date (filter full_30d_window = 1); Values: [Stickiness]; y-axis 0-15%
- Definition: DAU divided by distinct players active in the trailing 30 days; shown only once a full 30-day window exists.
- Source: `fact_daily_activity.csv (stickiness, clean_stickiness_window)`
- Insight (verbatim): Average stickiness is 9.9% on spike-free windows (from 2018-07-31): roughly one in ten monthly players plays on a given day.

## Page: Retention

### Day-N retention by weekly install cohort

- Visual: Matrix with background colour scale (light blue -> dark blue)
- Fields: Rows: cohort_week; Columns: day_n; Values: [Retention] formatted 0.0%; blank = censored
- Definition: Classic Day-N: share of a cohort active exactly N days after install. Only players with N days of data (eligible) count; empty cells are censored.
- Source: `fact_retention_cohort.csv (retained_users / eligible_users)`
- Insight (verbatim): D1 fell from 26.3% for June-July cohorts to 14.8% for September cohorts; the 2018-06-25 week is small because installs are missing on spike days.

### Retention curve, all cohorts

- Visual: Line chart with error band
- Fields: Axis: fact_retention_curve[day_n] (1-30); Values: [Retention Curve]; error bars [Retention CI Low]/[Retention CI High]
- Definition: Pooled retained / eligible for each day since install, with a 95% Wilson interval.
- Source: `fact_retention_curve.csv`
- Insight (verbatim): D1 21.5%, D7 5.5%, D30 2.1%. Excluding spike-day targets moves D1 only to 21.2%.

## Page: Revenue & LTV

### Monetisation by platform

- Visual: Table + clustered bar
- Fields: Rows: platform; Values: [Active Players], [Payers], [Revenue USD], [Payer Rate], [ARPU], [ARPDAU], [ARPPU]; totals row on
- Definition: Payer rate = payers / active players; ARPU = revenue / active players; ARPDAU = revenue / player-days; ARPPU = revenue / payers. IAP only.
- Source: `fact_monetization_platform.csv`
- Insight (verbatim): 27 purchases, $31.86 in total; remove_ads is 74% of purchases. Ad revenue is not in the export (1,912 rewarded-ad events carry no value).

### Cumulative IAP LTV per install

- Visual: Line chart (stepped)
- Fields: Axis: fact_ltv_curve[day_n]; Values: [LTV] (format $0.0000)
- Definition: Revenue from days 0..N after install / installs observable for 30 days (the same players at every N).
- Source: `fact_ltv_curve.csv`
- Insight (verbatim): LTV D30 is $0.0027 per install, from only 8 payers among 2,955 installs: directional, not a basis for UA bids.

## Page: Levels & difficulty

### Level funnel

- Visual: Column chart
- Fields: Axis: fact_level_funnel[level]; Values: [Reach Rate]
- Definition: Players who started each level / players who started level 1 (progression mode, all players active in the window).
- Source: `fact_level_funnel.csv (users_started)`
- Insight (verbatim): 27.8% of level-1 players reach level 10 and 7.8% reach level 30.

### Completion per attempt and quit rate

- Visual: Column chart (Completion per Attempt) + line (Quit Rate) on ONE percentage axis
- Fields: Axis: level; Column: [Completion per Attempt]; Line: [Quit Rate]
- Definition: Completion per attempt = level_complete / level_start. Quit rate = new players whose last level started is L / new players who started L.
- Source: `fact_level_funnel.csv (completes, starts, new_players_last_level, new_players_started)`
- Insight (verbatim): Level 27 is a wall: 4.5% of attempts succeed and 45.7% of new players who reach it stop there; levels 29 and 9 follow.

## Page: Churn model

### Churn prediction: ROC curve

- Visual: Line chart
- Fields: Axis: fact_churn_roc[fpr]; Values: tpr; Legend: model; add a y = x reference line
- Definition: Churned = no activity on days 7-30 after install. Features = install-day behaviour only. 75/25 stratified split, 5-fold CV.
- Source: `fact_churn_roc.csv, fact_churn_model_performance.csv`
- Insight (verbatim): Logistic regression reaches test AUC 0.68 (CV 0.62); the depth-4 tree 0.60. Churn rate is 72.1% of 2,955 installs.

### What predicts churn

- Visual: Bar chart (horizontal)
- Fields: Axis: feature (filter model = logistic_regression, top 8 by permutation_auc_drop); Values: [Permutation Importance]
- Definition: Permutation importance: drop in test AUC when one feature is shuffled.
- Source: `fact_churn_feature_importance.csv (model = logistic_regression)`
- Insight (verbatim): Install-day engagement time leads. The riskiest decile churns at 86.5% vs 51.3% for the safest: useful for ranking, weak for individual targeting.

## Page: A/B: observational tests and test design

### Observational comparisons (not randomised)

- Visual: Table
- Fields: fact_ab_observational_tests: comparison, metric, group_a, rate_a, group_b, rate_b, diff_a_minus_b, diff_ci_low, diff_ci_high, p_value, cohens_h
- Definition: Two-proportion z-test (= chi-square 2x2), 95% CI of the difference, Cohen's h. Traffic source compared within Android only.
- Source: `fact_ab_observational_tests.csv`
- Insight (verbatim): iOS D1 is -3.8 pp vs Android (p = 0.005, h -0.09); on Android, google-play installs lead (direct) by +6.8 pp (p < 0.001). Correlation, not causation.

### Sample size for a D1 retention A/B test

- Visual: Line chart (log y-axis)
- Fields: Axis: fact_ab_power_analysis[mde_abs]; Values: n_per_arm; tooltip days_needed_at_sample_volume
- Definition: Two-sided test, alpha 0.05, power 0.8, baseline = observed D1; run time = 2 x n / installs per day.
- Source: `fact_ab_power_analysis.csv`
- Insight (verbatim): Detecting +2 pp needs 6,834 players per arm: about 340 days at the sampled 40.3 installs per day. Run on full traffic or test bigger changes.

## Page: Data limitations

Text box, bullet list (verbatim):

- Sampled export: exactly 50,000 events per day. Absolute counts describe the sample; rates are reliable.
- 60% of players were acquired before the window; cohorts and LTV use installs inside it only.
- 29% of new players have no first_open and are excluded from cohorts.
- Only 27 purchases and no ad revenue: monetisation figures are directional.
- Traffic source is confounded with platform (iOS is (direct) only); app_remove exists only on Android and is not used as a churn label.
- Platform and source comparisons are observational, not causal.
