Data limitations

  • Sampled export: exactly — events per day. Absolute counts describe the sample; rates are reliable.
  • — of players were acquired before the window; cohorts and LTV use installs inside it only.
  • — of new players have no first_open and are excluded from cohorts.
  • Only — purchases and no ad revenue: monetisation figures are directional.
  • Traffic source is confounded with platform (iOS is (direct) only); app_remove exists only on Android and is not used as a churn label.
  • Platform and source comparisons are observational, not causal.
